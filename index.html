KD3CCO Ham Radio Engineering Log

A ham radio engineering log. Licensed in December 2025, so most of what is here is a first attempt at something experienced operators already know, written down while it was still fresh enough to include the wrong turns.

Each article records what was measured, how, and what the measurement does not establish. Where a later measurement overturned an earlier one, the correction is in the article rather than quietly replacing it. Scripts and raw sweeps are linked from the articles that use them.
