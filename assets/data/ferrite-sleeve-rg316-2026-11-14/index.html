Built assemblies, same day

Eleven four-core chokes wound and swept, with their own references taken at the build geometry rather than the single-sleeve one. The build geometry’s short reference measures 455 nH against the single-sleeve 289, so file 46 and not file 26 is the correct subtraction for files 47 through 58. All eleven were swept the same day, same fixture, same lead distances, so one reference covers them all.

The article numbers these 1 through 11 in the order wound. Chokes 1 to 3 were wound from a model since withdrawn; 4 to 11 vary one section at a time from a common base.

File 51 was not used. Resistance in ohms. A choke is resistance-dominant where R/|Z| is 0.707 or above, so only chokes 1 and 2 clear 5 kOhm on every band from 80 to 10 m while staying resistance-dominant. Choke 11 has the highest worst-band resistance of the eleven and is 81 percent reactive by magnitude at 10 m.

Seven of the eleven clear 5 kOhm everywhere from 80 to 10 m. The limiting band is 10 m for ten of the eleven.

These eleven are the evidence that no model in this work predicts a build. Summing the measured single sleeves of a choke misses by 40 percent on average; a section library solved from the assemblies themselves fits them to 1-8 percent but misses a held-out choke by 27 percent. Sections do add in series. The per-section values taken from bench sweeps do not transfer, because the bench dress is looser than any assembly’s.

Nothing below 20 m moved and everything above it did, which is stray capacitance and nothing else. This is now the best choke in the set: it beats the original on worst-band R, 6,858 against 6,706, and its limiting band moves from 10 m to 80 m.

Reproduce the figure with:

python3 assets/code/choke_s2p.py SJ000059.s2p --ref SJ000046.s2p \
  --xzero downward \
  --bands 1.9,3.5,7.1,10.1,14.2,18.1,21.2,24.9,28.4 --plot out.png

--ref-mode defaults to auto, which fits a delay and a lumped inductance to the reference and uses whichever matches. On SJ000046 it reports 453 nH lumped fits 1.9x better than a 4.35 ns delay and subtracts. Do not force divide on this fixture: the toroid article’s compact short is a 0.85 ns delay and wants division, this long-wire reference is not, and the two are not interchangeable.
