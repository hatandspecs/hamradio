File 51 was not used. Resistance in ohms. A choke is resistance-dominant where R/|Z| is 0.707 or above, so only chokes 1 and 2 clear 5 kOhm on every band from 80 to 10 m while staying resistance-dominant. Choke 11 has the highest worst-band resistance of the eleven and is 81 percent reactive by magnitude at 10 m.

Seven of the eleven clear 5 kOhm everywhere from 80 to 10 m. The limiting band is 10 m for ten of the eleven.

These eleven are the evidence that no model in this work predicts a build. Summing the measured single sleeves of a choke misses by 40 percent on average; a section library solved from the assemblies themselves fits them to 1-8 percent but misses a held-out choke by 27 percent. Sections do add in series. The per-section values taken from bench sweeps do not transfer, because the bench dress is looser than any assembly’s.

Reproduce the figure with:

python3 assets/code/choke_s2p.py SJ000059.s2p --ref SJ000046.s2p \
  --xzero downward \
  --bands 1.9,3.5,7.1,10.1,14.2,18.1,21.2,24.9,28.4 --plot out.png

--ref-mode defaults to auto, which fits a delay and a lumped inductance to the reference and uses whichever matches. On SJ000046 it reports 453 nH lumped fits 1.9x better than a 4.35 ns delay and subtracts. Do not force divide on this fixture: the toroid article’s compact short is a 0.85 ns delay and wants division, this long-wire reference is not, and the two are not interchangeable.

Not one of the eleven and not part of the design search. Wound as a 1:1 current balun to take a doublet’s 300 ohm twin lead into a wide-range tuner, and kept because it measures well as a choke and does not work as the balun.

Swept against the same reference, SJ000046, same fixture and lead dressing as the assemblies. Self-resonance 7.86 MHz, peak |Z| 14,930 ohm at 6.61 MHz, stray 0.60 pF from the R-L-C fit above 9 MHz.

The reference barely matters for this winding, unlike the single-sleeve sweeps: subtracting SJ000046 and using no reference at all agree within 0.3 percent on every band, because 453 nH is 81 ohms at 28.4 MHz against 8,400 of winding. Dividing the same reference gives −765 ohms at 28.4 MHz, unphysical, which is the delay-versus-lumped discriminator restated on an assembly.

Choke 13 is the best choke in the set, 8,587 ohm worst band over 80-10 m and resistance-dominant on all nine bands from 160 m to 10 m, against choke 2 re-dressed at 6,858 and 0.888. Three of the thirteen now meet both halves of the criterion: 1, 2 and 13.

Re-dressing alone (61 to 62) bought 22 percent at 10 m and cut stray C by 16 percent, but did NOT win dominance: 0.705 against the 0.707 line. Cutting capacitance also raises Rp, so conductance and susceptance fall together and the ratio barely moves. What won 10 m was the mix-43 section going 6T to 5T, which dropped Rp at 28.4 MHz from 17,938 to 14,045 and so raised the conductance 28 percent with the susceptance unchanged. Loss, not geometry.

SJ000062’s 160 m reading of 4,063 ohm is a bad sample, not a feature: 1.8 and 1.9 MHz dip two to three kilohms below their neighbors while 61 and 63 run smoothly through. All three carry |S11| > 1 over 1.0 to 2.26 MHz, which does not affect the series-through R and X but is a reason not to lean on 160 m.

Mix 43 at the top buys 10 m and costs the middle. Swapping it out for mix 31 at the same turn count raises 40 through 17 m by 9 to 13 percent and lowers 12 and 10 m by 5 to 6 percent, and the dominance ratio falls on every band above 40 m. That is the loss mechanism restated: mix 43 is lossier at 28 MHz, and dominance at the top is bought with loss.

Choke 14’s 10 m pass is inside the noise and should not be leaned on. Across the whole 10 m band rather than at its center, its worst R/|Z| is 0.7104 at 29.52 MHz, clearing the 0.707 line by 0.0034. Choke 13 at the same frequency is 0.7628. Choke 12 re-dressed failed the same line by 0.002. With build-to-build scatter still unmeasured, a margin of 0.0034 is not a pass that distinguishes itself from a fail. Choke 14 also goes reactance-dominant at 29.90 MHz, above the band edge but inside the sweep, where choke 13 holds 0.758 to 30 MHz.
